The six approved categories, owner images, 121-species/136-market-entity KPIs, 68 catalogue cards, routes, category memberships and canonical Trade Pulse/Production observations are preserved. The scope label remains dynamically derived from the completed period and included CN count.

Shared commercial cards now drive category views and the homepage Snapshot. A small generated homepage projection derives from the catalogue; there is no separately maintained species shortlist. Eleven entities currently have useful safe market metrics. Eligibility also accepts a independently verified price-only entity. Empty metrics are omitted.

Price selection is independent for each entity and metric. It checks public eligibility, source, methodology, verified scope and species attribution, then selects the newest valid observation without comparing its date with the global COMEXT period. Eight matching legacy price candidates were inspected. Their publication/quality flags do not establish a separately declared price geography and methodology for this catalogue projection. They remain withheld pending that evidence. Different trade volume scope or observation date alone is not an exclusion. No customs value/weight unit values were relabelled as representative prices. Thus zero catalogue cards presently show an eligible price; this limitation is explicit, not a fabricated price-coverage success. Legacy source observations remain unchanged.

Price history is a shared chart with a measure/unit title, €/kg axis, actual monthly/daily observation labels, accessible tooltip values, latest-available statement and representative-price explanation. Missing monthly slots have no bar; no continuous line, interpolation or forward fill is introduced. Legacy price routes retain their existing observations and source label while using the clearer chart. The current catalogue has no eligible price history; chart behavior is tested using clearly isolated fixtures, not published invented observations.

Trade cards explicitly label the 12-month mini-chart as EU internal trade volume in tonnes. Detail pages add labelled trade-volume histories, genuinely ranked top-five exporters and top-five bilateral corridors from the complete validated current-period dispatch observations. Current compatible legacy routes use the same corrected rankings. Numerical observations are not edited.
