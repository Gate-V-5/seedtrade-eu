# Trade and CN coverage V1

The 121-species audit adds 39 verified species-to-CN links across 32 species and 502 official COMEXT monthly bilateral observations on ten CN codes absent from the existing eleven-code market dataset. There are 40 distinct customs trade entities, with observations in 21. Existing Crop Master, 1,715 production observations, Trade Pulse values, representative-price engine, homepage species KPI and images remain unchanged.

Species-level trade is available for 11 of 121 species (9.09%): ten have full species-specific scope and maize (Zea mays) has partial scope. Newly supported species are soybean (Glycine max) and maize (Zea mays). The maize mapping includes separate grain-maize classes and hybrid sweetcorn; non-hybrid sweetcorn remains outside sowing-isolated coverage. No customs-class shares are inferred.

78 species have a defensible relationship to an observed customs entity. This is useful market discovery coverage, not proof of any individual species contributing volume. Group volumes are never copied into species observations. The 121-row intersection uses species-level trade: 11 production and trade, 71 production only, zero trade only, 39 neither. Group relationships are separate fields. Presence alone is not readiness for a commercial supply conclusion.

Official KSH 2025 and 2026 CN descriptions underpin the audit. Generic rice sowing CN 10061010 was reduced from species-confirmed to group scope: FAO recognises cultivated rice as Oryza sativa and Oryza glaberrima, while the CN text does not isolate either. Chickpea (Cicer arietinum), lentil (Lens culinaris), safflower (Carthamus tinctorius) and canary grass (Phalaris canariensis) legacy codes do not isolate sowing; those codes remain in the audit as excluded classification evidence. Original CN master and source observations were not overwritten.

New official data cover DE→PL, FR→PL, NL→PL and PL→DE dispatches during January 2025–June 2026. They are exporter-reported intra-EU corridors, not country totals, an EU total, imports, or extra-EU trade. Forty filtered requests yielded 502 paired euro/weight rows. Weight in source 100 kg units is multiplied by 100 explicitly. Nine source-reported zero-weight rows are retained with flags, not fabricated or treated as missing. Every new representative price is withheld. Seventy-five annual reported-month segments are available; only eleven contain twelve reported months. Partial segments are not annualised, interpolated or zero-filled.

The legacy manifest reports 56,741 PUBLIC_SAFE rows; its complete raw dataset was unavailable in the fresh clone and was not recounted. Added codes are disjoint from all eleven baseline market codes. Combined manifest-plus-new coverage is 57,243. The existing completed Trade Pulse subset of 35,238 and the 502 new rows total 35,740 separately; these counts must not be confused. Baseline market observations and numeric calculations are byte-preserved.

The Market page adds compact customs entity, seed category, evidence type, origin and period controls. Original species market pages remain linked. Existing group cards disclose multiple species. New data/code are route-loaded; new observations do not inflate the homepage data bundle. Official CN descriptions retain canonical English; controls and limitations have EN/DE/FR/ES/IT translations and fallback. Botanical names remain Latin.

## Limits and unresolved work

2024 KSH structure URL returned 404; no historical concordance is invented. Existing 2024 series retain their approved provenance. EUR-Lex HTML requested robot verification and was not bypassed; independently accessible official KSH tables were used. A multi-filter COMEXT query returned 413; ordinary individually filtered requests succeeded. The EU27_2020 partner filter returned empty dimensions and was never normalised. Bilateral country partners were verified instead. Ten species remain REVIEW_REQUIRED, thirteen lack a usable sowing-isolated mapping. End-use ambiguity for oilseed/vegetable/spice/flower cases remains explicit; no speculative code activation.

A repeated build left obsolete generated chunks; removing only reproducible dist output and running a clean build resolved the bundle check. Tests were rerun after the clean build: 244 Python and 34 Node cases pass, 45 routes prerender, 225 language/route DOM hydration combinations pass, new controls and Production controls pass. All B2B checks are mocks; zero real emails. Browser/mobile status is recorded in the publication receipt after deployment.

Target next research at the remaining verified CN entities lacking COMEXT data, broader country/flow coverage, official end-use/BTI evidence for the ten unresolved species, 2024 concordance, and the existing price-engine eligibility of the new observations. No commercial interpretation or supply forecasts were started.
