# Trade observation and validation v0.1

Local research implementation, September15 cycle. trade_observation_v01.py defines frozen TradeObservation with source/dataset/vintage/retrieval, reporter/partner/flow/month, nomenclature, Decimal quantities/value, definitions/status/provenance and secondary customs/transport/partner2 scopes. Explicit UNKNOWN required for unknown text. Monthly-only current scope. Provenance immutable tuple. No source adapters yet; caller must normalize codes, units/scales and verify definitions.

trade_validation_v01.py compares weight and value independently, requiring equivalent base dimensions and known methodology. Weight requires matching type/unit; value matching currency/basis. Missing/confidential/suppressed observations return NOT_COMPARABLE, with reasons. Zero reference/nonzero candidate yields undefined percentage and retained absolute difference; two zeros MATCH. Candidate-reference difference and absolute percentage relative to reference. Exploratory thresholds: <2 MATCH;2 through5 ACCEPTABLE;>5 through10 WARNING;>10 CONFLICT. They are not final business rules. Overall NOT_COMPARABLE can coexist with a usable per-metric comparison; inspect metric results rather than discarding them.

Vintages preserved and differences flagged, not silently discarded or required to have identical source-native IDs. Same-source comparisons flagged; upstream independence never assumed. No currency conversion, weight conversion, CN8 aggregation, quality score, signal or price calculation yet. Product descriptions retained but not compared as literal text; canonical edition/code must be independently verified.

No raw provider comparisons accepted yet.
