# Trade Intelligence mapping — CN 2026 research scope

Verified against Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1926, Article 2
and annex pages 125-126. Replacement annex applies from 1 January 2026.
Source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2025/1926/oj/eng
Local original: data/trade/CN_2026_official.pdf (1105 pages).

HS6 prefixes are recorded as parent groupings, not species-specific substitutes.
Hybrid ryegrass mapping remains unresolved; do not silently assign it to either
ryegrass CN8 category. Flax subheading has a preliminary-provision condition;
this research mapping does not determine customs eligibility or duty.

These are original 2026 annex definitions, not a historical 2024 mapping or a
complete check of later amendments. Before importing 2024/2025 trade data,
verify the applicable annual version and concordances. No trade values, masses,
prices or market shares obtained. No aggregate vetch/other-clover trade value
may be presented as the requested species' trade. Next: machine-readable mapping
with fail-closed year/species eligibility, tests, then Price Engine architecture.
