P7 — Crop Trade Basket v0.2
Extend the existing Crop Trade Basket architecture.
One SeedTrade crop may contain multiple CN/HS relationships.
Preserve:
DIRECT — sufficiently crop-specific for quantitative use.
PROXY — includes target crop plus other species/products; useful for code-level market trends, but the complete volume must not be attributed to the target crop.
CONTEXT — broader category useful as supporting market information.
Each relationship should preserve:
- nomenclature edition;
- jurisdiction;
- code;
- description;
- DIRECT/PROXY/CONTEXT;
- evidence source;
- confidence/evidence quality;
- validity period;
- known inclusions/exclusions.
Never invent species shares for mixed codes.
Preserve parent-child overlap protection to prevent double counting.
