SEEDTRADE.EU — Privacy Notice V1.0

**Owner-review draft. Not approved for publication. Effective date: to be set on approved publication.**

1. Information we use

SEEDTRADE.EU uses personal data to provide its professional B2B platform. Registration collects company legal name, registration country and number, registered address, business email, and the representative's first and last name. VAT details are optional where applicable. After email verification, you set a password, which is stored as a hash. Existing company profiles may also contain representative roles, phone numbers, business activities and species.

We record email verification, company-review decisions and evidence of Terms acceptance, representative authority and Privacy Notice presentation. When marketplace functions are enabled, we process listings, enquiries, requested quantities, replies, participants and dates, including commercial information you provide. Each listing's brokerage acceptance records the company, user, listing, terms version and hash, and time. An enquiry confirmation uses a single-use email link and the exact summary accepted by its sender. Only after confirmation is the enquiry and necessary contact information sent to MB Gate-V's configured coordination mailbox for manual brokerage. Business data can identify people, particularly named contacts and sole traders.

We also use account-security, session, verification and recovery records; notification delivery records; support, rights and moderation correspondence; and relevant request and error logs. The separate business-interest form collects company, email, role, country and submission time. We receive information from you, authorised company members, counterparties and service providers, and may use identified public or business sources for catalogue and listing information. Editorial photographs may include creator credits and source/licence references. Please avoid unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

Dedicated administrators use separate credentials and mandatory multi-factor authentication; their access and review conflicts are recorded. Administrator sessions last up to thirty minutes. These access limits do not determine when the related records are deleted.

2. Purposes and lawful bases

Our legitimate interests are providing professional accounts, reviewing company eligibility, enabling business offers and conversations, delivering service messages, preventing misuse, handling disputes and keeping the service secure and recoverable (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We use only information necessary for these purposes, subject to individuals' rights and reasonable expectations.

Contract necessity (Article 6(1)(b)) applies only where you personally are a party to the relevant contract, or request steps before entering it, and the processing is necessary. Your employer's contract does not by itself provide this basis for processing your data as its representative.

We process information to meet applicable legal obligations, including GDPR rights requests, required breach handling and binding lawful demands (Article 6(1)(c)). Required registration and eligibility information is needed for the relevant service; additional commercial details are optional. Terms acceptance is not GDPR consent or marketing permission. Company approval involves human review, not a solely automated decision with legal or similarly significant effects.

3. Who receives information

Eligible approved members see published offer information and seller company identity. Authorised company participants see their conversations. Private profiles, credentials and messages are not generally public. SEEDTRADE.EU Administration and service providers receive access only as needed for their tasks. Trading counterparties may be separate controllers of information they receive. We may disclose information where necessary for legal obligations, claims or safety.

If a seller expressly confirms public preview for a published offer, anyone may see its species, variety, origin country, quantity and unit, certification/category and availability. Public preview excludes prices, seller identity, direct contacts, private specifications and conversations.

Hostinger provides our hosting, database infrastructure and transactional email. Our documented recovery arrangements include encrypted database, application and private-configuration copies in Dropbox and restricted local storage. A copy can contain account and Marketplace records present when it is made; this does not mean that every later record has been uploaded to Dropbox. Dropbox's published privacy information describes possible processing in the United States and other countries, including by service providers. Primary hosting in the EEA does not by itself establish that all provider processing remains there. Contact [info@seedtrade.eu](mailto:info@seedtrade.eu) for information about recipients, processing locations and how to obtain a copy of applicable transfer safeguards.

4. How long information is kept

Retention depends on whether information is still needed for an account, business exchange, support case, legal duty, claim or proportionate security and recovery purpose. We review erasure requests manually and assess any lawful need to retain particular records. Closing an account or withdrawing an offer does not automatically erase counterparties' conversations or necessary acceptance evidence.

Verification links are valid for 30 minutes, password-reset links for 15 minutes and sessions for up to eight hours. These limits end their use; they do not mean every related record is deleted then. Password reset invalidates existing sessions. Logs and backup copies have separate retention considerations. The recovery procedure requires erasure decisions to be reapplied before restored service reopens. No fixed deletion or backup-rotation period is promised here.

5. Cookies and browser storage

A necessary secure session cookie keeps members signed in for up to eight hours. Your chosen language is stored in your browser until changed or cleared. Password-reset credentials are not saved in browser storage. Non-essential analytics and marketing tags are disabled.

6. Your rights

Subject to GDPR conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests; we must then stop unless we demonstrate compelling overriding grounds or need the information for legal claims. Contact [info@seedtrade.eu](mailto:info@seedtrade.eu). We verify requests proportionately and protect other people's information when responding.

We normally respond within one month. Any permitted extension of up to two further months is explained within the first month. You may complain to Lithuania's State Data Protection Inspectorate (VDAI) or another competent supervisory authority, including where you live or work. Material changes to this Notice will be communicated as appropriate. English is the authoritative legal language.
