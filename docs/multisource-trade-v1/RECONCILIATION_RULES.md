# Provider comparison and publication

Eurostat COMEXT DS-045409 remains the official EU aggregate baseline. Statistical observations use frequency, reporter, partner, flow, CN8 and period as their unique grain. Species links are metadata and never additional summation keys. Internal EU totals count export dispatches once and exclude mirror imports. Aggregate partner codes and special territories are excluded from country-grain totals and documented separately.

GlobalWits is an active secondary commercial source for this task, through the owner-supplied workbook pilot. Its 090961 customs group is spices including anise, badian, caraway, fennel and juniper berries, not a sowing-seed code. It never enters the Seed Trade Pulse. A supplier's name alone cannot attribute a biological species or sowing use. The pilot contains period-level country and company aggregates, not individually identifiable shipment records or shipment product descriptions. No species-identified shipment volume is inferred. Original formulas, labels and date conflicts remain in the private evidence layer. Raw workbook and contact information are never placed under src, public or tracked repository data. Publication rights are unknown: LICENCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED and PUBLICATION_RESTRICTED. An internal analytical permission is not a public aggregate/company-name licence.

Datamyne: ARCHITECTURE_READY_ACCESS_PENDING. No credentials, records or access are invented.

UN Comtrade is a possible independent international cross-check. Its public monthly HS120110 Germany-to-Poland June 2025 pilot returned zero records; absence is not zero trade. Numeric corroboration is therefore unavailable for this pilot. UN HS6 codes may combine national CN8 sowing and non-sowing subheadings. Never apply HS6 commodity totals to a sowing CN8 entity without a verified tariff-line distinction.

Before quantitative comparison, require compatible reporter systems, geographic coverage, partner attribution, period, flow, classification version and rank, net vs gross weight and currency. Imports typically use CIF values and exports FOB; source revisions, estimated quantities, trade systems and origin/final-destination attribution can differ. No assumption that providers must match, no USD/EUR comparison without an explicit dated conversion, and no automatic correction of the official primary record. Reconciliation retains both provider IDs and reports mismatches without merging or summing providers.
