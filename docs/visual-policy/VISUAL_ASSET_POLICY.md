# SeedTrade Visual Asset Policy V1

Effective17 October2026. The owner's Package1E.3A instruction is the authority for restoring the six historical GATE illustrations. This group-specific policy is canonical for visual selection. It does not change News factual/PUBLIC_SAFE gates or the existing News Experience V3 attribution placement.

| Protected group | Required image model | Replacement rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top News | Real, article-relevant photographs; exact commercial-use/modification/redistribution permissions, source, author and attribution evidence | Editorial relevance and exact-image rights review; never use AI/schematic substitutes or imply a stock image documents the event |
| Evidence for Commercial Seed Decisions | Three original owner-approved GATE AI illustrations | Preserve original hashes, existing mapping and placement; replacement requires explicit owner approval |
| Research & Partner Insights / Events | Partner/client-approved assets | Preserve requested placement and contextual publication permissions; approval is not a general stock or redistribution license |
| European Seed Market | Six historical original GATE AI category illustrations | Preserve historical originals, exact category mapping and full composition; replacement requires explicit owner approval |

The canonical inventory is CANONICAL_VISUAL_INVENTORY.json. It records each active/protected file, SHA256, category/article key, placement, rights model and original/derivative relationship. HISTORICAL_GATE_SOURCES.json records historical Git provenance and the current restoration authority. GATE_ENCODING.json records full-frame resize settings; GATE_VISUAL_REVIEW.json records derivative review. No new artwork is generated.

Owner-supplied GATE AI assets have owner-authorized use for this site. Do not label them CC0, Creative Commons, stock photography or documentary evidence. Do not invent generation-platform terms or a public reuse license. Preserve immutable original PNGs in source/recovery. High-resolution originals are omitted from the optimized build; active WebPs are resized without cropping, grading, overlays or recomposition. The existing16:9 boxes use contain, avoiding clipping at the original2048×1143 aspect ratio. Keep400/800/1200 srcset variants, existing sizes/breakpoints, meaningful localized ALT and neutral failures. The six-image transfer budget is strictly below1,000,000 bytes at each advertised width.

The six retired stock photographs, original JPEGs, derivatives and their actual CC0/CC BY-SA notices remain preserved as historical artifacts. Their licenses do not apply to restored GATE artwork. Active GATE provenance is shown separately; old license notices remain associated only with old stock filenames. Do not silently delete ShareAlike notices or relabel those photographs.

Top News and archived News are distinct. Existing schematic archive entries stay pinned for historical preservation, not eligible to replace photographs in Top News. No new schematic archive exceptions may be added to bypass policy. The current four homepage News images and all existing News mappings/rights evidence remain unchanged in this package. The News Photography Standard V1 still governs new News submissions; its mobile editorial-signoff requirements are not weakened by this catalogue-only release.

Before any visual change: identify its group, read full associated content, verify exact rights/authority, preserve originals/evidence, update the group inventory through the appropriate reviewed approval, generate full-frame derivatives where permitted, and run build/regressions. GATE replacements additionally require a new explicit owner instruction. Preserve partner placement requests and News attribution. A file name, image-search thumbnail, matching username, old approval for another image or general site policy is not permission.

The build guard pins the canonical inventory checksum and verifies every protected asset/mapping, active GATE registry, original/derivative hash, category mapping, dimensions, budget and contain framing. There is no skip/force flag. Mutation tests demonstrate changed image bytes, wrong mappings, altered original identity and unreviewed inventory changes fail closed. Updating the inventory and its independently pinned checksum is a deliberate reviewed source change; these source controls do not replace repository access controls or claim to stop a person who can deliberately rewrite the guards.

QA must separate static/hash/DOM results from actual browser/device evidence. Check all five languages, group placement, loaded sources, crops/full-frame composition and fallbacks. Never claim mobile or full WCAG compliance without actual testing. Save source diffs, review authority, test/build outputs, browser evidence, release identity and rollback in the connected Dropbox checkpoint before/after a scoped release. No global licensing rule may override these four distinct models.
